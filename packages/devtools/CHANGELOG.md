# @ogygia/devtools

## 0.14.0

### Minor Changes

- [#606](https://github.com/ogygiajs/ogygia/pull/606) [`1fdd1a0`](https://github.com/ogygiajs/ogygia/commit/1fdd1a0d39f1f8a1fb2c3c0615695faec7350c0a) Thanks [@PuruVJ](https://github.com/PuruVJ)! - - `@ogygia/devtools`: breaking: the dock no longer installs `window.__ogygia_testing` or loads a testing engine of its own. `@ogygia/testing` injects its engine into every page; list `testing()` from `@ogygia/testing/vite` in the app under test (the dock alone still feeds an injected engine).

- [#760](https://github.com/ogygiajs/ogygia/pull/760) [`90a7449`](https://github.com/ogygiajs/ogygia/commit/90a744948a95004c40088f00f3774406293f320b) Thanks [@PuruVJ](https://github.com/PuruVJ)! - devtools: in production the profiler accepts only a secret of at least 16 bytes; a shorter one keeps its pages closed and says so (`devtools_profiler_secret_short`)
  devtools: the profiler allows ten wrong keys per address in 15 minutes (the login form, `x-profiler-key` and `x-profile` share the count), then answers 429 until the window passes
  devtools: a profiler login is a random session id the server signs; it expires after 12 hours, logout ends it, and any change to it is refused (everyone logs in once again after the upgrade)
  devtools: the profiler's beacon flag cookie belongs to one login: it ends when that session expires or logs out

- [#1192](https://github.com/ogygiajs/ogygia/pull/1192) [`b8d8cf0`](https://github.com/ogygiajs/ogygia/commit/b8d8cf08bc70d5040917c39dd8ae05ec5853d17e) Thanks [@PuruVJ](https://github.com/PuruVJ)! - The profiler's `/page`, `/reset`, `/replay/<id>` and `/logout` take a POST only, so a link on another site can no longer change the profiler's state; a GET answers 405 with `Allow: POST`. The documented call is the same query sent as a POST: `POST /__profiler/page?p=/slow/page&runs=6&format=json&key=<secret>`. A run page opened from another site waits for a click before it profiles.
